The SVG `<title>` and `<desc>` match the corresponding discovery `imageAlt`; arrows, curves, layers, and molecular motifs are schematic only where the primary source does not provide a literal scene. Each asset was reviewed at full size and in 1440 × 560 desktop and 390 × 360 mobile cover crops on 2026-07-17.
