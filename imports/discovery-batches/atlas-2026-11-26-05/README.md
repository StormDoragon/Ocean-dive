# Atlas update 05

Seven independent exoplanet research findings span astrometry, radial velocities, transits and direct imaging. Closely related findings from one paper are grouped by their research result. Where a discovery team published a preprint before the final paper, the record identifies the early report and links the peer-reviewed publication as well. Map markers are labeled Earth-based observatory, archive or research proxies.

The artwork consists of original editorial diagrams. Production publication dates will be assigned at release in UTC.
