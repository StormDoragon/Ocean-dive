# Atlas update 05

Seven independent exoplanet research findings span astrometry, radial velocities, transits and direct imaging. Closely related findings from one paper are grouped by their research result. Where a discovery team published a preprint before the final paper, the record identifies the early report and links the peer-reviewed publication as well. Map markers are labeled Earth-based observatory, archive or research proxies.

The artwork consists of original editorial diagrams. Production publication date is 2026-11-26 UTC.

The combined release of batches 05–07 had 210 articles and 632 unique sitemap URLs. The local production build returned HTTP 200 for all 632 destinations and preserved every one of the 609 public URLs. All 16 addition canonicals and original images passed. The East Java proxy hub was noindex and outside the sitemap.

In Chrome at 390 × 844, the homepage, flat map and timeline rendered without horizontal overflow. The East Java marker opened the exact article by keyboard. Mobile-UA compressed HTML grew from 41,325 to 42,281 bytes on the homepage, 18,395 to 18,981 on the map and 227,229 to 238,928 on the timeline, comparing production with the local production build.
